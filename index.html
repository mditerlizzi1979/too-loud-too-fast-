About My Collection

Welcome to "Too Loud. Too Fast." - My personal Hot Wheels collection showcase! I've been collecting these amazing die-cast vehicles for over a decade, and my passion for them grows stronger every day.

My collection features over 1,250 rare and classic Hot Wheels from various series spanning multiple decades. Each car has its own story, from lucky finds at estate sales to strategic trades with fellow collectors.

This site is dedicated to showcasing my passion for these incredible miniature vehicles. Whether you're a seasoned collector or just starting your journey, I hope you enjoy browsing through my collection as much as I enjoy collecting them!
